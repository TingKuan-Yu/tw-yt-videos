### 第 1 章節：房市冷清沒生意，買方想出價房仲卻愛理不理？ / Chapter 1: Why Ignore a Buyer in a Slow Market?

現在房市這麼冷，很多房仲都在喊沒有生意，結果，竟然有房仲，客戶都已經想買房子了，他卻愛理不理？！\
The housing market is so slow that many agents are complaining about having no business, yet some agents barely respond even when a client already wants to buy a home!

最近，我剛好看到 PTT 的文章，真的蠻有意思的，有個網友看上了一間房子，屋主開價 1,038 萬。\
Recently, I came across an interesting post on PTT: someone had found a home they liked, and the owner was asking NT$10.38 million.

他去查了實價登錄，發現同社區半年前才成交 900 萬出頭，所以，他就跟房仲出價 910 萬。\
They checked actual transaction records and found that a home in the same community had sold for just over NT$9 million six months earlier, so they offered NT$9.1 million through the agent.

結果，你知道房仲怎麼回他嗎？\
Do you know how the agent responded?

直接跟他說：「太低了啦，屋主不可能賣你！」\
The agent simply said, "That's too low; the owner would never sell to you at that price!"

後來這個買方願意加到 920 萬，甚至說要正式下斡旋，結果房仲竟然已讀不回，根本就懶得理他！\
The buyer later offered to raise the price to NT$9.2 million and even wanted to submit a formal offer through the brokerage's negotiation process, but the agent read the message and never replied!

更奇怪的是，這一間房子後來還從售屋網站上面下架，但是那個買方調查後卻說，房子根本就沒有賣掉啊！\
Even more strangely, the home was later removed from the listing website, but the buyer said that, after looking into it, they found it had not been sold!

這到底是怎麼回事呢？\
What on earth was going on?

真的非常奇怪，對不對？！\
It's really strange, isn't it?

### 第 2 章節：殘酷現實：你付服務費，房仲就一定站在你這邊嗎？ / Chapter 2: Does Paying a Fee Put the Agent on Your Side?

不過先申明喔，今天我不是要幫買方講話，也不是要替仲介護航。\
Let me make this clear first: I'm not here to take the buyer's side or defend the agent.

買方出價 910 萬，到底合不合理？\
Was the buyer's NT$9.1 million offer reasonable?

我們等一下也會來討論一下。\
We'll discuss that in a moment.

但是你不覺得，嫌人家出價低是一回事，不過客戶想要出價都不理他，又是另外一回事？\
But don't you think rejecting a low price is one thing, while ignoring a client who wants to make an offer is another?

所以，今天我們就來聊聊，這背後到底是怎麼回事呢？\
So today, let's talk about what might be going on behind all this.

首先，房仲到底在幫誰呢？\
First, who is the agent actually helping?

你找他看房子、跟他討論價格，很自然會覺得說，他應該是來幫我買房的吧？！\
When you ask an agent to show you homes and discuss prices, you naturally think they're there to help you buy a home, right?

尤其是想到，買房你還要付他一筆服務費，你一定更希望房仲幫你談一個便宜的價格，對不對？\
Especially when you remember that you also have to pay a service fee, you would certainly hope the agent could negotiate a lower price for you, wouldn't you?

可是，如果我們把畫面切到屋主那一邊，想法肯定是剛好相反的。\
But if we switch to the owner's perspective, the thinking is bound to be exactly the opposite.

他也會想說：「我有付服務費，你應該幫我賣個好價格吧！」\
The owner would also think, "I'm paying a service fee, so you should help me get a good selling price!"

你看，這是不是很尷尬呢？\
You see how awkward that is?

買方想要省錢、賣方想要多賺錢，中間卻是同一家仲介在溝通。\
The buyer wants to save money, the seller wants to earn more, and the same brokerage is communicating between them.

而且你不知道屋主到底有多急著賣。\
And you don't know how urgently the owner needs to sell.

屋主也不知道，你是真的只想出這個價格嗎？\
The owner also doesn't know whether this is really the only price you're willing to offer.

還是，其實還能再加呢？\
Or could you actually offer more?

仲介夾在中間，往往比你們任何一邊都更清楚，現在談到哪一個階段了，有沒有機會接近成交了！\
The agent in the middle often knows better than either side how far negotiations have progressed and whether a deal is getting close!

### 第 3 章節：揭秘房仲心態：為什麼你願意出價，他卻懶得幫你談？ / Chapter 3: Why an Agent Might Not Negotiate Your Offer

所以，如果以實務經驗來講，仲介不一定偏袒誰，但是，你一定要分清楚，他是想促成一筆交易，而你想要買一個划算、負擔得起的房子，這個是兩回事哦！\
So, speaking from practical experience, an agent doesn't necessarily favor either side, but you need to distinguish their desire to close a deal from your desire to buy a good-value home you can afford!

仲介希望成交，不代表他的利益跟你的利益會完全一樣。\
An agent's wish to close a deal does not mean their interests are exactly the same as yours.

那重點就來了，既然成交才能賺錢，你都願意出價了，他為什麼還可以不積極幫你談呢？\
That brings us to the key question: if agents only earn money when a deal closes, why wouldn't an agent actively negotiate for you when you're ready to make an offer?

是他覺得真的沒有機會？\
Does the agent think there is genuinely no chance?

還是你的價格讓他覺得不值得花時間呢？\
Or does your price make the negotiation seem not worth the time?

你想想看，房仲每天可能要帶好幾組客戶看房子，他的時間也是成本哦！\
Think about it: an agent may show homes to several groups of clients every day, and their time has a cost too!

所以，假設屋主開價 1,038 萬，心裡就是希望至少賣 980 萬，結果今天剛好有個買方跑出來談 910 萬，整整差了 70 萬。\
Suppose the owner asks NT$10.38 million and hopes to get at least NT$9.8 million, but a buyer comes along offering NT$9.1 million, leaving a gap of NT$700,000.

房仲可能就會想說，這個價格屋主根本就不可能接受，我花時間去談，最後也是白忙一場。\
The agent might think the owner would never accept that price, so spending time negotiating would ultimately achieve nothing.

所以，他乾脆把時間留下來，給那一些比較願意加價、比較有機會成交的買方。\
So the agent simply saves that time for buyers who are more willing to raise their offers and more likely to close.

這其實就是在篩選客人。\
This is effectively a way of screening clients.

### 第 4 章節：房仲不願透露的店內生態：什麼叫做把房子留在手上賣？ / Chapter 4: Keeping a Listing Within One Brokerage

但，還有另外一種可能，就是房仲可能想把這個房子留在自己手上賣。\
But there's another possibility: the agent may want to keep the sale within their own operation.

這個部分就比較是房仲的生態了，有機會我們再細聊。\
That has more to do with how the brokerage business works, and we can discuss it further another time.

簡單的說，他很可能就是不太願意讓其他房仲、或其他的買方去介入，為什麼呢？\
Simply put, the agent may be reluctant to involve other agents or buyers; why might that be?

因為如果買方跟賣方都由自己服務的話，就有機會二邊的服務費，都在自己的店裡成交。\
Because if the same office serves both the buyer and the seller, it may be able to keep the service fees from both sides.

當然啦，我不是說這個案例，房仲一定是這樣子。\
Of course, I'm not saying that this is definitely what the agent in this case was doing.

畢竟沒有人有證據，所以也不能隨便亂講。\
After all, nobody has evidence, so we can't make unsupported accusations.

但是我覺得這件事情最奇怪的地方，根本不是買方出了 910 萬太低，而是人家後來都加價到了 920 萬了，也明確表示想要正式下斡旋，房仲竟然還可以已讀不回。\
But to me, the strangest part isn't whether NT$9.1 million was too low; it's that the agent still left the buyer on read after the buyer raised the offer to NT$9.2 million and clearly asked to make a formal offer.

你覺得這樣合理嗎？\
Do you think that's reasonable?

房仲可以覺得價格太低，也可以告訴買方屋主不願意賣，但問題是，這個價格到底有沒有真的拿去跟屋主談呢？\
The agent can consider the price too low or tell the buyer the owner won't sell, but was the offer actually discussed with the owner?

還是從頭到尾都只是房仲自己覺得不可能成交？\
Or was it simply the agent's own assumption all along that no deal was possible?

這才是我覺得這一件事情最值得討論的地方。\
That's what I think is most worth discussing about this case.

所以，下次當房仲跟你說：「這個價格屋主不會賣啦！」\
So next time an agent tells you, "The owner won't sell at that price!"

### 第 5 章節：房仲說「屋主不可能賣」，教你用一招測出這句話是真是假！ / Chapter 5: Ask Whether the Owner Actually Rejected the Offer

你先不要急著相信，也不用急著跟他爭辯。\
Don't rush to believe it, and don't rush to argue either.

因為這一句話有二種可能，第一種，房仲真的跟屋主確認過了，屋主就是不願意降價。\
There are two possibilities behind that statement: first, the agent really checked with the owner, and the owner simply refuses to lower the price.

但第二種呢？\
But what about the second possibility?

可能就是房仲自己覺得價格太低，根本沒有拿去跟屋主談。\
The agent may personally think the price is too low and may never have discussed it with the owner.

那你要怎麼分辨呢？\
How can you tell the difference?

其實也很簡單，你可以直接問他說：「這個是屋主親自拒絕的呢？還是，你覺得他不會接受呢？」\
It's actually simple: ask directly, "Did the owner personally reject this offer, or do you think they wouldn't accept it?"

當然，比較好的做法，你也可以用正式的要約書或是斡旋的方式，提出你的價格。\
Of course, another, better approach is to submit your price in a formal written offer or through the brokerage's negotiation process.

記得把出價的金額、條件、期限，還有跟房仲的對話紀錄，都保留下來。\
Remember to keep records of the offer amount, conditions, deadline, and your conversations with the agent.

因為，口頭嘴炮講一講，跟正式提出文件、購買條件，這是兩回事！\
Because talking casually is very different from formally submitting documents and purchase conditions!

當然，正式出價一定有法律效力，所以，內容一定要看清楚再簽。\
Of course, a formal offer definitely has legal effect, so be sure to read its contents carefully before signing.

但是講到這裡，我就要提醒大家一件事情了。\
But at this point, I need to remind everyone of something.

### 第 6 章節：拿實價登錄殺價一定買便宜？小心這盲點讓你反而買貴！ / Chapter 6: The Limits of Recorded Transaction Prices

我們前面一直在討論房仲有沒有問題，不過，接下來我要講的，可能很多買方不愛聽。\
So far, we've been discussing whether the agent did anything wrong, but many buyers may not like what I'm about to say next.

因為，這個買方拿實價登錄，出 910 萬，真的就代表他出價合理嗎？\
Does offering NT$9.1 million based on transaction records really mean that the buyer's offer was reasonable?

你想想看，這個買方為什麼敢出 910 萬呢？\
Think about it: why did this buyer feel confident offering NT$9.1 million?

因為，他查到同一個社區半年前有一間房子，900 萬出頭就成交了，而且，只差兩個樓層哦。\
Because they found that a home in the same community had sold for just over NT$9 million six months earlier, only two floors away.

所以他就會覺得：「我出 910 萬，應該很合理吧！」\
So they would think, "Offering NT$9.1 million should be quite reasonable!"

但是我跟你講，這可能就是很多人買房最容易犯的錯誤！\
But let me tell you: this may be one of the most common mistakes people make when buying a home!

你可能會以為，同一個社區房價就應該差不多嗎？\
You might assume homes in the same community should cost roughly the same.

那還真的不一定！\
That really isn't necessarily the case!

比如說，一間有車位、一間沒有車位，價格能一樣嗎？\
For example, can one home with a parking space cost the same as one without?

一間採光好、屋況漂亮，另一間漏水、還要花錢整理，你覺得能直接比嗎？\
If one home has good natural light and is in great condition while another leaks and needs renovation, do you think you can compare them directly?

甚至，同一個大樓，樓層、面向不同，價格都可能也會差很多。\
Even within the same building, different floors and orientations can make a big difference to the price.

更何況，你看到的實價登錄，只是半年前的那一筆成交。\
Besides, the transaction record you're looking at is just one sale from six months ago.

所以我一直跟大家講，實價登錄是參考的答案，而且，它是落後的指標，不是標準答案。\
That's why I keep saying transaction records are a reference point and a lagging indicator, not a definitive answer.

但，還有一件更重要的事情，你以為拿實價登錄殺價，就一定能夠買到便宜嗎？\
But there's something even more important: do you think negotiating with transaction records always gets you a bargain?

我跟你說，搞不好剛好相反，你可能還會買貴了。\
Let me tell you, the opposite might happen: you could end up overpaying.

### 第 7 章節：房市下跌期出價大忌！為什麼看過去行情會多當冤大頭？ / Chapter 7: How Past Prices Can Lead to Overpaying

為什麼我說，看實價登錄買房，反而可能讓你買貴呢？\
Why do I say that buying based on transaction records could actually make you overpay?

我舉一個例子，你就懂了。\
Let me give you an example, and you'll understand.

假設半年前，同一個社區有一間房子成交 1,000 萬，結果現在房市變冷了、買方變少了。\
Suppose a home in the same community sold for NT$10 million six months ago, but the market has since cooled and there are fewer buyers.

有些屋主急著用錢，開始願意降價了，搞不好人家心裡已經願意 900 萬就賣了，可是，你就是不知道啊！\
Some owners urgently need cash and are becoming willing to lower their prices; one might already accept NT$9 million, but you simply don't know!

你看到實價登錄 1,000 萬，就覺得自己出了 950 萬，已經殺了 50 萬，很厲害了，對不對？\
You see a recorded price of NT$10 million and feel that offering NT$9.5 million means you've negotiated an impressive NT$500,000 discount, right?

結果呢？\
And what happens?

你可能還比屋主願意接受的價格，多出了 50 萬。\
You may still be offering NT$500,000 more than the owner would accept.

這就是很多買方容易忽略的問題。\
This is a problem many buyers overlook.

實價登錄告訴你的，是過去成交多少，不代表這一間房子現在值多少，更不代表屋主願意賣多少。\
Transaction records tell you what sold for how much in the past, not what this home is worth now, and certainly not what its owner would accept.

所以，房市上漲的時候，你拿過去的價格去出價，可能永遠買不到！\
So when the market is rising, offering past prices may never get you a home!

但是，房價下跌的時候呢？\
But what about when housing prices are falling?

你還拿過去的高價當標準，那就可能真的買太貴了！\
If you still use past high prices as your benchmark, you might really overpay!

更可怕的是什麼呢？\
What's even more worrying?

### 第 8 章節：房仲說「已經有人出更高了」！ / Chapter 8: Claims of Higher Competing Offers

當房仲跟你說，已經有人出更高的時候，你會不會一緊張，就自己加價了呢？\
When an agent says someone has already offered more, will you get nervous and raise your own offer?

好，我們再切回剛剛那個 PTT 的案例，買方原本出 910 萬，後來願意加到 920 萬。\
Let's return to that PTT case: the buyer originally offered NT$9.1 million and later agreed to raise it to NT$9.2 million.

結果房仲跟他說，已經有好幾組客人出到 950 萬了，屋主底價是 980 萬。\
The agent then said that several other buyers had already offered NT$9.5 million and that the owner's minimum was NT$9.8 million.

請問如果是你，聽到這句話會不會開始緊張了呢？\
If you heard that, would you start getting nervous?

心裡想說：「完了，這間房子我真的還蠻喜歡的，萬一被別人買走了，怎麼辦呢？」\
You might think, "Oh no, I really like this home; what if someone else buys it?"

「那我是不是乾脆出 960 萬，搶在別人前面呢？」\
"Should I just offer NT$9.6 million to get ahead of everyone else?"

你看，原本你只想出 920 萬，現在卻開始考慮 960 萬，一下子就多出了 40 萬。\
You see, you originally only wanted to offer NT$9.2 million, but now you're considering NT$9.6 million, an increase of NT$400,000 all at once.

這在談判裡面，就叫做定錨效應。\
In negotiation, this is called the anchoring effect.

房仲丟出 950 萬、980 萬兩個數字，你的注意力可能就被拉到這個價格區間了。\
The agent throws out NT$9.5 million and NT$9.8 million, and your attention may get pulled into that price range.

但問題是，那幾組 950 萬的買方到底是不是真的存在呢？\
But do those buyers offering NT$9.5 million actually exist?

我們根本就無法確定。\
We simply can't be sure.

當然，也不能直接說房仲在騙人。\
Of course, we can't simply say the agent is lying either.

可是你要記住，有人出 950 萬，不代表你就應該出 960 萬哦！\
But remember, someone else's NT$9.5 million offer doesn't mean you should offer NT$9.6 million!

買房最怕的，就是因為擔心被別人搶走，結果自己越加越高。\
One of the biggest risks in buying a home is raising your offer higher and higher because you're afraid someone else will get it.

那如果今天換的是我，我會怎麼跟房仲談呢？\
If it were me, how would I negotiate with the agent?

### 第 9 章節：買房談判高手的反向操作：一句話直接破解催促加價！ / Chapter 9: Responding Without Automatically Raising Your Offer

我跟你講，我絕對不會跟房仲說，有人出 950 萬，就急著加價。\
Let me tell you, I certainly wouldn't rush to raise my offer just because the agent said someone had offered NT$9.5 million.

我反而會跟房仲講說：「喔，如果已經有人出 950 萬了，那這樣很好，你們先趕快去跟那一組客人談。」\
Instead, I'd tell the agent, "Oh, if someone has already offered NT$9.5 million, that's good; please go ahead and negotiate with that buyer first."

為什麼我要這樣講呢？\
Why would I say that?

因為，我買房子最在意的，不是有沒有搶贏別人，而是這一間房子到底值不值得我出這個價格。\
Because when I buy a home, what matters most isn't beating someone else to it, but whether the home is worth that price to me.

而且，我也不會傻傻的跟房仲講說，我最高可以出到多少。\
And I wouldn't foolishly tell the agent the highest price I could pay.

因為，實際上你可能都根本搞不清楚，買下這間房子，你會花下多少成本。\
In fact, you may not even have a clear idea of the total cost of buying the home.

像我，會把附近的成交行情、屋況，還有需要整理的費用，都算清楚，再提出我願意接受的價格跟條件。\
For example, I work through nearby transaction prices, the home's condition, and renovation costs before putting forward a price and terms I can accept.

所以，如果房仲還是說太低，那沒有關係，我就繼續再看下一間。\
So if the agent still says it's too low, that's fine; I'll keep looking at other homes.

房子一大堆嘛，大繼承潮、少子化，這兩個議題被炒得火熱，那我就覺得很奇怪了。\
There are plenty of homes, and with so much discussion of the coming wave of inheritance and declining birth rates, I find this strange.

房子又不是只有這一間，幹嘛非買不可呢？\
This isn't the only home out there, so why must you buy it?

## Content Notes and Recap 內容導讀與摘要

### 先看案例中的異常回應 / Start with the unresponsive agent

案例中，屋主開價 1,038 萬，買方從 910 萬加到 920 萬並想正式出價，卻被已讀不回。房子下架後是否成交，只有買方調查後的說法；講者關注的核心是回應不清楚。

In the case, the asking price was NT$10.38 million. The buyer raised an offer from NT$9.1 million to NT$9.2 million and wanted to make it formal, but received no reply. The claim that the delisted home remained unsold came from the buyer's account. The speaker focuses on unclear communication.

### 區分成交誘因與買方利益 / Separate closing incentives from the buyer's interests

買方想省錢、賣方想賣高價，仲介則希望促成交易。講者提出時間成本與保留店內成交兩種可能解釋，但沒有證據確認案例仲介的動機。

Buyers want a lower price, sellers want a higher one, and agents want a transaction to close. The speaker offers time costs and keeping a sale in-house as possible explanations, but does not establish the agent's motive in this case.

### 問清楚誰拒絕了出價 / Clarify who rejected the offer

先問是屋主親自拒絕，還是仲介的判斷，再保留正式出價的金額、條件、期限與對話紀錄。來源對法律效力的說法較絕對，實際仍須檢視具體文件與適用條件。

Ask whether the owner rejected the offer or the agent merely expects rejection. Keep the amount, conditions, deadline, and conversation records. The source makes a categorical statement about legal effect; the actual position still requires checking the particular documents and conditions.

### 把歷史價格放回比較條件 / Put past prices back into context

同社區不代表屋況、車位、樓層與面向相同，半年前成交也不代表現在底價。講者用假設說明：以 1,000 萬歷史成交為標準，出 950 萬，仍可能高於屋主願收的 900 萬。

Homes in one community can differ in condition, parking, floor, and orientation. A sale six months ago does not reveal today's minimum price. In the speaker's hypothetical example, an offer of NT$9.5 million based on a past NT$10 million sale could exceed an owner's willingness to accept NT$9 million.

### 辨識定錨而不臆測欺騙 / Recognize anchoring without assuming deception

仲介提到其他買家出 950 萬及底價 980 萬，可能讓原本願出 920 萬的人開始考慮 960 萬。其他買家是否存在無法確定；講者主張以價值與成本判斷，而不是只求贏過別人。

Claims of NT$9.5 million competing offers and a NT$9.8 million minimum may draw a buyer from NT$9.2 million toward NT$9.6 million. The existence of those buyers is uncertain. The speaker argues for judging value and costs instead of simply trying to beat others.

### 看總價如何影響長期負擔 / Connect purchase price with longer-term affordability

從 910 萬到 980 萬增加 70 萬；在貸款八成的假設下，自備款增加 14 萬、貸款增加 56 萬。買來出租也須留意租金未必跟著上漲，不能只看成交當下的興奮。

Moving from NT$9.1 million to NT$9.8 million adds NT$700,000. With 80% financing, the extra down payment is NT$140,000 and the extra loan is NT$560,000. Rental income may not rise with the purchase price, so the excitement of closing is not enough.

### 按同一假設比較方案 / Compare scenarios under the same assumptions

來源示範用 Deal Tools 比較 910 萬、950 萬與 980 萬，假設利率 2.5%、30 年、三年寬限期與月租 23,000 元。理解重點是比較寬限期前後負擔；來源未列最終月付金，不能把提問中的 5,000 或 10,000 元當答案。

The source demonstrates Deal Tools comparisons at NT$9.1 million, NT$9.5 million, and NT$9.8 million, assuming 2.5% interest, a 30-year term, a three-year interest-only period, and NT$23,000 monthly rent. The focus is comparing affordability across the two payment phases. No final monthly figures are supplied; NT$5,000 and NT$10,000 are questions, not results.

### Q1

先辨認轉換譯文

本課來源是中文，英文是轉換譯文。先讀中文確認意思，再遮住英文試著表達，最後比較用字與搭配；不要把英文誤稱為講者的原始措辭。

Recognize translated learning material

The source is in Chinese, and the English is a translation. Read the Chinese for meaning, try expressing it yourself, and then compare the wording and collocations. Do not treat the English as the speaker's original wording.

用確認問題區分資訊來源

練習把兩件事問開：屋主真的拒絕，還是仲介認為不會接受。以 Did the owner reject the offer? 確認事件，再用 Is that the owner's response or your assessment? 追問資訊來源，避免直接指控。

Ask where a claim comes from

Practice separating an owner's rejection from an agent's assessment. Ask, Did the owner reject the offer? Then clarify, Is that the owner's response or your assessment? This checks the source of the information without making an accusation.

把確定性放進文法選擇

比較 may think 與 thinks，以及 does not mean 的否定範圍。讀句子時先圈出情態助動詞，再說明哪些是觀點、哪些仍不確定；完整規則與快速檢核請使用文法與句構頁。

Use grammar to control certainty

Compare may think with thinks, and notice what does not mean actually denies. Mark modal verbs before explaining which claims remain uncertain. Use the grammar page for the full rules and quick checks.

前往文法與句構課程

準確讀出房價與增額

把 910 萬對應為 nine point one million，70 萬對應為 seven hundred thousand。先說單位，再說增加的是總價、自備款還是貸款；不要把價格差當成每月房貸差。

Read prices and differences accurately

Match NT$9.1 million with nine point one million, and NT$700,000 with seven hundred thousand. State whether a difference concerns the price, down payment, or loan. A price difference is not a monthly payment difference.

用成本理由設定界線

用 Our offer reflects the renovation costs. 說明出價依據，再用 We are not prepared to raise it. 設定界線。兩句都是教學情境，不代表案例買方真的說過，也不承諾對方會接受。

Set a boundary with a cost-based reason

Explain your basis with, Our offer reflects the renovation costs. Then set a boundary with, We are not prepared to raise it. These are practice lines, not quotations from the case, and they do not guarantee acceptance.

先口頭重述，再交叉核對

用三句英文重述：仲介的回應、過去成交資料的限制、自己的成本界線。錄音後檢查時態、金額與 may 等保留語氣，最後只回查不確定的一句，不重複背誦整頁。

Retell first, then cross-check

Retell the material in three sentences: the agent's response, the limits of past records, and a cost-based boundary. Record yourself and check tense, amounts, and hedging words such as may. Revisit only the sentence you are unsure about.

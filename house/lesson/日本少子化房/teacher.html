學習目標

先把價格、流動性與費用分成三個概念，再用英文各寫一句定義。不要背下每個未查證數字；本課要練習的是概念區分與可重用表達。

Learning Goals

Separate price, liquidity, and expenses, then write one English definition for each. Do not memorize every unverified number; this lesson develops conceptual distinctions and reusable language.

延伸練習

搭配與意義

把 demand has shifted、costs have skyrocketed 和 find someone to take over 當成完整搭配練習。先說出它們在本課的功能，再換成中性情境，避免只記單字短譯。

Collocations and Meaning

Practice demand has shifted, costs have skyrocketed, and find someone to take over as complete chunks. Explain their function in the lesson, then move them into neutral situations instead of memorizing isolated translations.

延伸練習

來源導向文法

本課文法重點是描述變化與限制結論。先比較 has shifted、were determined 和 may exceed 的功能，再到文法頁做快速檢核；保留 may，才能避免把預測說成保證。

Source-Grounded Grammar

The grammar focus is describing change while limiting conclusions. Compare has shifted, were determined, and may exceed, then use the grammar page's quick checks. Preserving may helps keep a forecast from becoming a guarantee.

延伸練習

朗讀與節奏

先用只讀英文模式聽一句，再停下來指出對比或因果連接詞。on one hand 與 on the other hand 適合分成兩個意群，不需要臆測原講者的口音或實際發音。

Reading and Rhythm

Listen to one sentence in English-only mode, then pause and identify contrast or cause-and-effect signals. Treat on one hand and on the other hand as separate sense groups without guessing the original speaker's accent or pronunciation.

延伸練習

情境應用

在虛構討論中，練習直接詢問貸款可行性，再以 Could you explain whether 改成委婉請求。內容要保持中性，不把課程例句當成對真實物件的推薦。

Situational Application

In a fictional discussion, ask directly about financing, then soften the request with Could you explain whether. Keep the content neutral and do not treat practice sentences as recommendations about real properties.

延伸練習

複習與自查

最後用三句英文說明一個概念、一個對比與一項待確認資料。檢查句子是否保留條件與可能性，再回到全文核對原句；英文自然化版本只能作教學，不能取代來源。

Review and Self-Check

Finish with three English sentences: one concept, one contrast, and one item needing verification. Check conditions and uncertainty against the reader. Naturalized teaching versions must not replace the source wording.
